FAQ

Common questions

Not sure where automation fits in your business? Start here, or ask us directly.

What can AI automation do for my business?

It takes repetitive tasks off your team: replying to common enquiries, capturing customer details, confirming bookings and sending reminders. That means less admin, fewer missed enquiries and more time for your clients.

Can it help with enquiries outside business hours?

Yes. Enquiry automation can respond and capture the key details when your team isn't available, so potential bookings aren't lost overnight or at weekends.

Can automation help me get more repeat customers?

Follow-up messages, reminders and rebooking prompts make it easy for clients to come back, and help reduce missed appointments along the way.
